Privacy Policy

VM All Video Downloader

Effective date: 21 October 2026

This policy explains what information VM All Video Downloader (“the app”) handles and why. The app is published by VMT Developer (“we”, “us”).

In short
  • You don’t need an account, and we don’t run any servers. We don’t collect, store or sell your personal information.
  • What you save, browse and copy stays on your phone.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some data to show those ads, as explained in section 2.

1. Information that stays on your phone

Saved videos and photos

Files you save are stored in the StatusSaver folder inside your phone’s Download folder. We never receive them.

Websites you open

The app’s built-in browser loads pages straight from each website. If you sign in to a website, you sign in with that website, not with us. The sign-in and the website’s cookies are kept in the app’s browser storage on your phone. The app uses a website’s cookies only to download a video you asked to save from that website, and sends them only to that website’s own servers.

Searches

If you type words in the address bar instead of a web address, they are sent to Google Search to show you results.

Copied links

While the app is open, it checks whether text you copy is a web link, so it can offer to open it or show a notification about it. It doesn’t keep or send what you copied.

WhatsApp statuses

If you allow access to the WhatsApp Statuses folder through Android’s folder picker, the app reads that folder to show the statuses you have already viewed on this phone, and copies the ones you choose to your Download folder. Nothing is uploaded.

Settings

Your language and other preferences are stored on your phone.

2. Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob, a service of Google: banner ads on some screens, and a full-screen ad that can appear when you open the app, when you come back to it, or just after you confirm a download (you can close it to continue). To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device’s advertising ID;
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location can be worked out;
  • information about your device and the app, such as phone model, Android version and app version;
  • how you interact with ads, and crash and performance data from the ads code.

Google may use this information to show personalised ads where the law allows it and, where required, you have agreed.

Your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent with Google’s consent form before any ads load. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy settings.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off personalised ads, in your phone’s settings (usually Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

To learn more, see the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Google Play

The app uses Google Play’s in-app updates to tell you when a new version is available. That check is handled by Google Play under the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Permissions

  • Internet and network access: to load websites, download files and show ads.
  • Notifications: to show download progress and to tell you when you copy a link the app can open.
  • Foreground service: so a long video can keep downloading after you leave the app.
  • Storage (older Android versions only): to save files and show them in Downloads.
  • WhatsApp Statuses folder: only if you grant it, as described in section 1.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob, as described in section 2.

5. Sharing

We don’t sell, rent or share personal information, because we don’t collect any. The only company that receives data through the app is Google, for ads and Google Play as described above. Websites you open in the app receive information from your visit under their own privacy policies.

6. Keeping and deleting your information

We keep no information about you, so there is nothing for us to delete. On your phone:

  • Delete saved files in the app’s Downloads screen or with any file manager. Saved files stay on your phone after you uninstall the app, until you delete them.
  • Clear browsing data, sign-ins and settings by clearing the app’s storage (Settings → Apps → VM All Video Downloader → Storage → Clear storage) or by uninstalling the app. This also removes the app’s access to the WhatsApp Statuses folder.
  • For data Google collects for ads, use Google’s controls at My Ad Center.

7. Security

The app has no servers of its own, so we hold none of your information. Connections to websites use HTTPS whenever the website supports it.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, for example under the GDPR in Europe or California law, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to object to its use. We don’t hold personal information about you; requests about ad data are best made to Google through the links in section 2. You can contact us with any question about your privacy.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date at the top.
